ABOUT

A City That Inspires

Viña del Mar is Chile's most iconic coastal destination, known for its golden beaches, lush gardens, vibrant culture and relaxed yet sophisticated atmosphere. Whether you're looking to unwind by the ocean, explore local flavors, or discover nearby treasures like Valparaíso, Viña del Mar offers the perfect blend of natural beauty and urban charm.

Beautiful Beaches

Enjoy golden sands, crystal-clear waters and a vibrant coastal atmosphere.

Stunning Gardens

Explore world-famous gardens, colorful flowers, and green spaces throughout the city.

Local Gastronomy

Savor fresh seafood, Chilean wines and a rich culinary scene by the sea.

Nearby Valparaíso

Just minutes away, discover a UNESCO World Heritage city full of art, history and colorful streets.
