About

A city that inspires

Viña del Mar is a Chile's most iconic coastal destination, known for its golden beaches, lush gardens, vibrant culture and relaxed yet sophisticated atmosphere. Whether you're looking to unwind by the ocean, explore local flavors, or discover nearby treasures like Valparaíso, Viña del Mar offers the perfect blend of natural beauty and urban charm.

Beautiful Beaches

Enjoy golden sands, crystal-clear waters and a vibrant coastal atmosphere.

Local Gastronomy

Savor fresh seafood, Chilean wines and a rich culinary scence by the sea.

Stunning Gardens

Explore world-famous gardens, colorful flowers, and green spaces throughout the city.

Nearby Valparaíso

Just minutes away, discover a UNESCO world Heritage city full of art, history and colorful streets.
